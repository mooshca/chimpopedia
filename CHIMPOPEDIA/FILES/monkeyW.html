Monkey World

What is Monkey World?

Monkey World is a primate rescue and rehabilitation centre in Dorset in England. It is home to over 250 primates they rescued from all over the world from many different situations. One of Monkey Worlds biggest aims is to end the illegal pet trade, between 1989 and 2014 they had rescued 78 primates from the British Pet Trade and continue to this day to safe more.

Hours of Operation

Monkey World is open 10am until 5pm every day, excluding Christmas day. The last entry is an hour before closing.
